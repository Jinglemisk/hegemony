# Sim parity audit: cards, ventures and the Assembly

Branch `feat/v2` at 52ed7af. Read-only. Scope: #85 year deck, #86 player deck and ventures,
#87 Assembly, #93 reach audit, #75/#76 luxuries and the Port.

**How the numbers were got**

- Batch figures: `step17-batch.json` (40 games, rotated slaver/civic/trader/master).
- "Probe": four `npx tsx` scripts in `sim-audit/probe-cards/`, 7 to 17 s each. They play one
  partial game (seed 1000, the batch's first table) up to an Assembly opening and print what
  the Assembly heuristic scores there. `policiesCopy.ts` is a scratch copy of
  `src/sim/policies.ts` with its private functions exported; the repo is untouched.
  One game, three sittings (years 6, 10, 12): the numbers show mechanism and scale, not rates.
- Line numbers: `policies.ts` = `src/sim/policies.ts`, `telemetry.ts` = `src/sim/telemetry.ts`,
  engine files under `src/game/`.

**Suspicions, answered**

1. Laws read through a base number: refuted for income, hunger, prices and happiness (all go
   through engine selectors). Confirmed in three small places: Land Reform (frontier terms),
   Manumission and Master Builders (`ideaOpportunityValue`). The larger problem is different:
   the Assembly scores a Law on a static position, so a price, a growth limit, a rider or a
   vote rule scores exactly zero (rows 16 to 31).
2. Stratokles and rival harm: confirmed (rows 1 to 4).
3. Merchant Convoy: confirmed. Table 0, 0, 2, 2, 2, 4 on a 2-gold stake, 5/3 back. The bot
   assumes nothing; it scores every face through the real transition (row 43).
4. Assembly commands: all eight are reachable and used. Three narrowings, two documented
   (rows 5 to 8).
5. Year cards honoured only for an uncollected seat: confirmed, and it matches the engine
   (row 35).
