# Sim parity audit: National Ideas, setup, victory, personalities

Branch `feat/v2` at 52ed7af. Read-only. Evidence comes from the code, from the Step 17
batch (`step17-batch.json`, and its turn CSV at `../s17/batch.csv`), and from short
probes in `sim-audit/probes-isp/` (each under 15 s, none plays a whole game):

- `draft-agg.ts`: replays setup for the batch's 10 seeds x 4 rotations and scores every
  open Idea at each of the 160 draft picks. It reproduces the batch's 160 picks exactly.
- `draft.ts`: the same for one game, with the projection deltas.
- `purchase.ts`: what a second Idea is worth at years 4 and 6 of one game.
- `dole.ts`: the value of one more food a year, with and without Public Dole.
- `trader.ts`: the trader's commands in years 1 to 3 of one game, and five single turns.
- `adjacent.ts`: whether each seat's founding colony can ever be upgraded.
- `twotitles.cjs`: reads the batch JSON only.

Counts: BUG 3, GAP 20, STALE 6, OK 23.

## Answers to the five suspicions

1. **Same three Ideas in 40 of 40 games.** Half true. The three are taken every game, but
   by different seats: slaver takes Civic Tradition 21 and New Settlers 19; civic takes
   Public Dole 25, City Pioneers 8, Civic Tradition 4; trader takes City Pioneers 32 and
   Slave Colonies 8; master takes Public Dole 15, Civic Tradition 15, Slave Colonies 10.
   The draft is scored with the personality's weights on the real post-pick state, plus
   Step 9's hand-set opportunity numbers. It behaves as a fixed ranking per personality
   because each personality opens identically in all 40 games (slaver c1 f0 s3 + s2,
   trader c1 f2 s1 + f1 s1) and six Ideas score a constant. Rows 14 and 2 to 13.
2. **Trader at 13 to 18%.** A bot problem first. Rows 31, 32, 9, 33, 29. One rules
   question remains: a freeman fed from the bank makes 1 gold and eats 2 gold of food.
3. **37 of 40 by the deck.** Confirmed, and the cause is the bots. A seat reaches two
   titles in 36 games, three in 6, and wins by the race in 3. All 34 seats that ended on
   two titles ended in a game where at least one title was held by nobody. Rows 26, 23, 24.
4. **Four weak Ideas.** Holds for Treasury Grant and Capital Works. Does not hold for
   Urban Planning (scored 0.0 in 66 of 160 draft decisions) or Harbour Planning (0.0 in
   25 of 160). Rows 4, 5, 11, 12.
5. **Weights naming a changed mechanic.** None. `PERSONALITY_WEIGHTS`, `chooseIdea` and
   `ideaOpportunityValue` have not changed since #90. The food reserve is the one constant
   still keyed to hunger at income. Rows 33, 37, 49.
