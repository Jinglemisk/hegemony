Scope: #82 (005edc9), #83 (d76b92a), #84 (fac45fb), #92 (efc1614), #99 (52ed7af), read at `feat/v2` 52ed7af.
Method: code reading, the Step 17 batch report, and three single-turn probes (no game loops) in
`sim-audit/probes-economy-unrest/` (`probe1-reserve.ts`, `probe2-growth-and-unrest.ts`, `probe3-import-and-idle.ts`).
"Probe" in the evidence column means one of those runs showed it. "Unverified" marks an inference.

Score units used below: `evaluateSmart` multiplies pop weights by 10 and materials by 10/8, so for the
master weights a slave is 12, a freeman 20, a citizen 30, a gold 1.25 (`policies.ts:49-59, 627-632`).

## The five suspicions

1. Food reserve overbuys after #99: **confirmed** (reserve row). Target is next printed food deficit plus 2, per unit 14. It no longer makes sense: the purchase can wait a turn at the same price.
2. Projection order: **refuted**. Hunger then unrest, on the current turn and every projected one. What it leaves out is arrivals and purchases (rows 1 and 4).
3. Unrest model reads today's thresholds and terms: **confirmed OK** for the lines, the level, tokens and calm's expiry. The price of a riot is the gap (row 3).
4. Scorer numbers from before #83: the luxury weight (sized against v1's Port), the riot charge of 50 (July), and the move bundles. Buildings, growth, moves and the bank are otherwise priced by the engine through `transition`.
5. Riot rule: not sound on expected value under the current table, and it decides little, because seats reach the table spent (insurance row).
