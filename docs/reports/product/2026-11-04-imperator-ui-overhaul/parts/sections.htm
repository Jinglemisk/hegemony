Short version

Imperator's bottom-leftThe settlement panel appears only while a province is selected and sizes itself to it: a large two-part panel for your own province, a small card for anyone else's. With nothing selected the corner is empty map.
OursOur bottom-left panel never leaves and holds five jobs at once: the verbs, the realm header, three realm ledgers, an overview that repeats the top bar, and the selection. Each one gets too little room.
The new shellThe selection owns the bottom-left corner and carries what you do to a place. Realm drawers open off the left rail and join it in one block. The menu opens windows you can move; the outliner and the dock hold the right edge. Anything that spends asks once.
What it costsThe shell's layout and its panel components change. The engine, the map and the alarms stay as they are. Eight slices, each one shippable alone.

Prototype

Our real board, the late-game dev scene with Kyros to play. Proposal is the new shell. Proposal B is a copy of it with your 4 November changes: the log line across the dock, a centred rail with larger buttons, drawers the outliner’s width, and drawer contents built from icons. Today is the live build in the same state. Everything on the screen responds to a click. Rest on a tooltip until its bar fills, then hover an underlined word. Enter says yes to a confirm, Esc closes the top-most thing, keys 1–4 switch modes and H opens the hand.
