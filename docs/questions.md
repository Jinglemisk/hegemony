## Q51 — When is the additional starting Pop granted?

**Context:** National Ideas are drafted after capital and colony placement, but Idea #7
says “Start with one additional Pop.” Patching placement counts would change a placement
that has already happened and would make the setup workflow disagree with the rule text.

**Options:** Grant one player-chosen Pop immediately when the Idea is drafted; move the
Idea draft before placement; or reauthor the Idea as a first-turn growth benefit.

**Recommendation:** Grant one player-chosen Pop to a legal owned settlement immediately
after the draft pick. This preserves the settled post-placement draft and makes the effect
an explicit canonical command/result rather than retroactive setup math.

## Q52 — What does Maximum Colonies +1 mean in the current game?

**Context:** The current `maxColoniesPerTile` limits colonies sharing one tile; there is
no per-player maximum colony count. Applying Idea #6 to that existing field would mean
“three colonies may share a tile,” not the PDF's apparent national expansion limit.

**Options:** Introduce a per-player colony supply/cap and increase it by one; increase the
per-tile sharing cap; or reauthor the Idea as a different expansion benefit.

**Recommendation:** Introduce an explicit per-player colony supply/cap, with its baseline
number selected against the physical board and normal full-game colony counts. The Idea
adds one to that cap; the per-tile sharing rule remains unchanged.

## Q53 — How does the retired Luxury Trader Idea work?

**Context:** Idea #10 starts with a Luxury Goods' Trader, but the Trader and all land
luxuries were removed. Luxuries now require coastal Ports and represent unique physical
assets, while a player's capital is not guaranteed to be coastal.

**Options:** Make the player's first Port free; reserve or immediately grant one luxury;
require a free Port in the capital even when inland; or replace the Idea entirely.

**Recommendation:** Make the first legally built Port free and let it claim normally.
This preserves the Port race, topology, and one-owner invariant without inventing an
off-board luxury or an inland exception.

## Q54 — Does the Capital slot Idea stack with the existing bonus?

**Context:** Capitals already receive +2 building slots. Idea #4 also says “+2 Building
slots in the Capital,” which would produce +4 above an ordinary settlement and may be a
much larger advantage than the other drafted Ideas.

**Options:** Stack the full +2; reduce the Idea to +1 additional slot; or reinterpret the
Idea as guaranteeing a fixed total rather than adding slots.

**Recommendation:** Use +1 additional Capital slot. It remains visibly asymmetric without
doubling an existing signature bonus; validate the exact value in the matched Idea campaign.
