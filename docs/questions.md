## Q77 — Which happiness model ships at Step 5?

**Context:** Two models are on the table. The clamped bank keeps today's stored happiness
number, as whole numbers between −10 and +10, and events add or remove points. The level
stores nothing: each turn happiness is Temples + 2 per luxury − half your slaves − Unrest
tokens, +2 if you bought calm this year, with riot at −3 and revolt at −6. Bad events place
Unrest tokens that stay until a riot or a Festival clears them. The 2026-09-06 ruling built
the bank first and the level only if riots persisted. But five v2 cards and the winning
shell's happiness display are written for the level, and the audit's rerun suggests
removing the food debt alone halves riots, so the level might never be triggered.

**Options:** Build the level at Step 5; the cards and the mock work as drawn, at the cost
of the plan's largest engine change. Or build the bank and rewrite the five token cards as
point gains and losses; cheaper, and the mock's level display becomes a bank display.

**Recommendation:** The level. It is the paper's legibility core, happiness you can read
off the board, and the shell already shows it.
