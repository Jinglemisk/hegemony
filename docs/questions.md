## Q85 — Does Trade keep the Port chips?

**Context:** Since #160 each luxury good's icon stays on its raft in every mode, with its
holder's seat mark under it. Trade also hangs a chip off each Port naming the good it holds,
so a held good is named twice in Trade, and the chip can crowd the good's name
([MAP-2](plans/ui-remaining.md#map-2--trade-names-a-held-good-twice)).

**Options:**

- A. Drop the Port chips; the raft's icon and seat mark say who holds what.
- B. Keep them; they say which Port holds the good.

**Recommendation:** A.

## Q87 — Does a traded luxury good need the receiver's own Port?

**Context:** The owner ruled on 2026-10-31 that a luxury good counts only while its holder has a
Port of their own beside it, on one of the good's two tiles, and that a good whose Port is
destroyed goes back to the board unclaimed ([agency package](plans/agency-package.md), ruling 7).
Trade can still hand a good to a seat with no Port beside it. Today a traded good counts for the
receiver at once, and a Port can only be built beside an unclaimed good.

**Options:**

- C. A traded good counts once the receiver has their own Port beside it. A Port may then be built
  beside a good its holder owns, the one place the Port-building rule widens.
- D. Goods stop being tradable.

**Recommendation:** C. It applies the ruling with no exception and keeps goods in trade. A good is
worth most to a seat with a settlement beside it, so goods would trade mostly between neighbours.
