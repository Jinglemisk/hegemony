## Q85 — Does Trade keep the Port chips?

**Context:** Since #160 each luxury good's icon stays on its raft in every mode, with its
holder's seat mark under it. Trade also hangs a chip off each Port naming the good it holds,
so a held good is named twice in Trade, and the chip can crowd the good's name
([MAP-2](plans/ui-remaining.md#map-2--trade-names-a-held-good-twice)).

**Options:**

- A. Drop the Port chips; the raft's icon and seat mark say who holds what.
- B. Keep them; they say which Port holds the good.

**Recommendation:** A.

## Q87 — What happens to a luxury good when its Port is gone?

**Context:** The owner ruled on 2026-10-31 that a luxury good counts only while its holder has a
Port of their own beside it, on one of the good's two tiles
([agency package](plans/agency-package.md), ruling 7). If that Port is destroyed (a riot's fire,
or a rival's city driving off the colony that held it), the good's +2 happiness goes, and a Port
elsewhere does not help. Today a claimed good stays its holder's for the game and counts with no
Port, a Port can only be built beside an unclaimed good, and a traded good counts for the receiver
at once. Two things follow that the ruling does not settle.

**Options:**

For a good whose Port is lost:

- A. It stays the holder's, inactive, until a Port of theirs stands beside it again. A Port may
  be built beside a good its holder owns for this.
- B. It goes back to the board, unclaimed; the first Port beside it claims it, the old holder's
  included.

For a good received by trade:

- C. It counts once the receiver has their own Port beside it. A Port may be built beside a good
  its holder owns for this.
- D. Goods stop being tradable.

**Recommendation:** A and C. Under A a lost Port costs the happiness until a 4 gold, 2 stone Port
is rebuilt, and one good keeps one owner. Under B a rival whose city drove off your colony holds
that tile and can claim the good with a Port there. C applies the ruling with no exception: a good
is worth most to a seat with a settlement beside it, so goods trade mostly between neighbours.
