## Q85 — Does Trade keep the Port chips?

**Context:** Since #160 each luxury good's icon stays on its raft in every mode, with its
holder's seat mark under it. Trade also hangs a chip off each Port naming the good it holds,
so a held good is named twice in Trade, and the chip can crowd the good's name
([MAP-2](plans/ui-remaining.md#map-2--trade-names-a-held-good-twice)).

**Options:**

- A. Drop the Port chips; the raft's icon and seat mark say who holds what.
- B. Keep them; they say which Port holds the good.

**Recommendation:** A.

## Q86 — The development cards feel lackluster

**Context:** The owner, 2026-10-28: "i pay 2 gold to buy a devcard and it gives me 1 wood?"
The most common card costs 2 gold, two turns of a freeman who eats a food each turn, and
gives +1 of a material, one turn of a slave. That is the removed bank's price, 2 gold a
material, at random: the instant cards average 0.73 materials a draw, and with the held
cards a draw is worth about one material. 14 of the 40 cards are +1 yields that land on the
draw with no choice. The [card list](plans/development-deck.md#the-card-list) waits on this,
and slice 1 waits on the card list.

**Options:**

- A. Yields grow with the realm (Foraging: +1 food for each settlement), so a card stays
  worth its gold as realms grow.
- B. Fewer instant cards, more held cards played at a chosen moment (a build discount, a
  free pop move, one tile harvesting twice), so every card is a decision.
- C. Keep the list; let the balance batches tune the price and the counts.

**Recommendation:** A, with B's strongest held cards in place of some +1 yields.
