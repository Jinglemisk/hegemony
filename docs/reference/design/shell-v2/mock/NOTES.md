# s-hybrid-arc · s-hybrid's bar, board and end turn, with p-ledger-r3's arc sheet

The bottom and bottom-left are now p-ledger-r3's: the realm sheet with its ledger body and curved edge, the six discs, their names and the fans. s-hybrid's verb strip is gone. Everything else is s-hybrid unchanged: the bar with the purse, the omen card and the consult icons, the rival discs with their hover tooltip, the ticker tab, the board with its raster seals, the label floor and the fit, and the end turn at bottom right.

- **Restyled, not pasted**: the sheet is bone like the bar, with s-hybrid's rules (ink-12 between cells, ink-30 under the tabs, a clay underline on the open tab). Discs are s-hybrid's ivory disc with a 2px ink ring that turns clay on hover. Options are s-hybrid's small bone disc on a hairline. Disc names and fan labels use s-hybrid's raised paper (ivory on a hairline), so they read the same on the sheet and over the sea. The HUD's single drop-shadow and hairline outlines the sheet, the discs and the fans. Prices follow s-hybrid's rule of numbers and resource icons with no words. An exchange shows what you pay, then what you get, for example 3 wood ▸ 1 gold. Icons stay inside s-hybrid's set of 16, 24, 32 and 48.
- **Geometry**: the r3 sheet at 1280 and 1440, 528 wide (s-hybrid's own 1440 width) with a 196 arc, 48px discs, inset 36 and pitch 113.6. At 1920 it moves to s-hybrid's tier: 672 wide (s-hybrid's own), a 212 arc, 64px discs carrying 48px icons, inset 44 and pitch 141.0. Both keep r3's rule that every box gap between neighbouring discs is over 64. The 1280 narrowing to 480 is gone, because six discs need the length.
- **Groups, fans and keys** are r3's: the same `GROUPS` and `DISCS` data, two-level fans for Build and Exchange, 500ms dwell and 250ms linger, hotkeys 1 to 6, Escape backing out one level. The sixth disc is Grow, and swapping it is a one-line change to `DISCS`. Fans avoid the ticker and the end turn as well as the bar. All 13 fans were checked at all three widths: none go off-screen or touch chrome, a disc or a name.
- **End turn** keeps its disc, badge, label and tooltip, but loses the stretch of strip it stood on, since the strip was part of the removed verb row.
- **Realm body** is r3's ledger, not s-hybrid's page. The per-column Grow and Promote buttons and the build-option row are gone because the fans carry those verbs. The head is seal, "Realm of Damon", and the National Idea on one line; at 1920 its stacked name and price overflowed the row. Slots follow s-hybrid's data (capital 4), so ARGOS reads "2 of 4 slots" to match its board pips.
- **Board fit** is s-hybrid's, with every disc and name added as an exclusion box. **Island: 53.0 / 59.6 / 54.8%** at 1280 / 1440 / 1920 (s-hybrid 39.9 / 47.8 / 43.6; p-ledger-r3 42.4 / 54.0 / 51.0). The strip and the rail are both gone, so the island gets the width.
- **Gate shots**: `hover-fan` (Build), `hover-fan-2` (Build, then Freemen), `hover-exchange` (Exchange, then Wood), `hover-rival`.
